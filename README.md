Entry for the [Fortune Cookie for the Liquidated Trader](https://make.fun/competitions/u-clever-harbor-75-fortune-cookie-for-the-liquidated-38fd239c) competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## What it does

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes a single fortune-cookie line for a
crypto trader who was just liquidated, to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Fortune favors the bold, but your liquidation favored the exchange.

One line, 10 words, 67 characters — inside the competition's gate of 1–160
characters and at most 20 words. The line keeps the classic fortune voice,
names the loss outright, and lands the joke on a double meaning: the bold
are favored by fortune, but this trader's liquidation favored the exchange.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
